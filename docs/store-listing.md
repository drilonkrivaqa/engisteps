# EngiSteps store listing draft

## Name

EngiSteps

## Short description

Learn circuits step by step, practice with feedback, and use engineering tools.

## Full description

Know how to start. Understand each step. Solve the next one yourself.

EngiSteps helps you work through circuit fundamentals with guided examples and
short practice activities. Choose the right method, calculate your answer, and
get feedback that helps you understand what to try next.

Start with three topics:
- Series circuits: combine resistors along one path.
- Parallel circuits: recognize branches and find equivalent resistance.
- Voltage dividers: calculate the unloaded output across a resistor.

Each topic includes one guided example and four practice problems. Use hints when
you need them, reveal a worked solution, and revisit problems you completed with
help. Your progress distinguishes guided work from practice solved independently
on your latest attempt.

The engineering workbench includes 26 tools across mathematics, circuits,
physics and more. Enter expressions, convert units, save calculations, keep
favorites, write study notes and organize study tasks.

No account required. The installed mobile app works offline. Learning progress,
notes and calculations are stored on your device. There are no ads or subscriptions.

The current learning course covers introductory resistor networks, not a complete
engineering curriculum. Calculators use simplified models and show relevant
assumptions. Data does not synchronize between devices.
