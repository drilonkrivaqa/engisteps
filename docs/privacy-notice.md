# EngiSteps privacy notice — technical draft

Prepared for the current implementation on 24 September 2026. Before publishing,
the owner must add their publisher identity and support/privacy contact and host
the reviewed notice at a public URL. This file is not a claim that store or legal
requirements have been completed.

## Information stored by the app

EngiSteps stores learning activity progress, use of hints/corrections, calculator
history, favorites, notes, planner entries and display preferences locally on the
device or in the browser's site storage. The app does not require an account.

## Transmission and third parties

The current app has no developer-operated backend, advertising SDK, analytics
SDK, cloud synchronization or AI service. It does not send learning answers,
notes or calculations to a server. Copying working places that text on the
system clipboard; the user controls where it is pasted.

The web edition requires a web host and may fetch application/runtime assets.
The chosen hosting provider and infrastructure may process normal web request
information. Add the actual hosting provider and its practices before publishing
the web edition. Do not claim that the web edition makes zero network requests.

Device or browser backups and synchronization are controlled by the user's
operating system and browser settings, rather than by an EngiSteps account.

## Retention and removal

Local data persists until removed. Calculator history is limited to 200 entries.
Clearing app storage or browser site data removes saved app work. Back up anything
needed first. Uninstall and backup behavior depends on the platform.
